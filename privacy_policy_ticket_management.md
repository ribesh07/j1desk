J1Soft ("we," "us," or "our") provides the Ticket Management System application (the "App") to help teams manage projects, tickets, tasks, assignments, comments, and work progress.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may collect, how it is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to users. By using the App, you acknowledge this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled and how your organization uses the App, we may collect:

- **Account information:** name, email address, profile image, user ID, and authentication-related information.
- **Work and ticket information:** projects, ticket titles and descriptions, task details, statuses, priorities, assignments, labels, due dates, comments, and activity history.
- **Files and attachments:** documents, images, screenshots, and other files you choose to upload to tickets or comments.
- **Device and technical information:** device type, operating system and app version, diagnostic information, and logs needed to maintain and troubleshoot the App.
- **Notification information:** notification preferences and, if push notifications are enabled, a device push-notification token.
- **Usage information:** interactions with App features and information needed to understand errors, improve performance, and maintain security.

We do not ask users to provide sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for their organization’s use of the App. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in ticket descriptions, comments, or uploaded files unless your organization has authorized you to do so.

We use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts and authenticate users.
- Provide project, ticket, task, team, assignment, comment, attachment, and activity-history features.
- Send in-app or push notifications about assignments, comments, status changes, reminders, and other relevant activity.
- Display dashboards, reports, search results, and team workloads.
- Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
- Prevent misuse, investigate security incidents, and enforce applicable terms or policies.
- Respond to support requests.
